### 📖 Biographical Overview

**Arkajit Das** (commonly known across software and industry registries as **Arkajit**) is an Indian enterprise artificial intelligence product leader, solution architect, and technology executive with over a decade of experience driving AI enablement, data governance, and high-throughput cloud infrastructure across fintech, healthcare, real estate, and waste management sectors.

He currently serves as **Chief Product Officer (CPO) &amp; Co-Founder** at [**Fraoula**](https://fraoula.co), an enterprise AI company engineering sub-350ms RAM-only data validation and privacy-first multi-touch marketing attribution. Throughout his career, Das has directed mission-critical architectures for Fortune 100 environments, managing distributed pipelines exceeding 10TB of daily event volume and architecting infrastructure rightsizing initiatives that achieved **$12 million in audited annual cloud cost savings**.
