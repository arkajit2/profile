## Biography & Executive Focus

Arkajit Das is a technology executive, systems builder, and author based in Bangalore, India, working with engineering teams worldwide. He serves as Co-Founder and Chief Product Officer at Fraoula, directing high-speed data verification platforms and privacy-preserving marketing attribution engines.

Over a decade of systems architecture, he has led enterprise software initiatives across Computer Associates (CAI), PayPal (via eClerx), Clean Harbors, and Deloitte. His engineering work eliminated $12 million in annual recurring cloud server waste, automated over 10,000 hours of banking settlement labor, and maintained 99.5% continuous uptime across industrial networks.

He graduated as University Batch Valedictorian (Rank 1) in Computer Applications from West Bengal University of Technology (WBUT / MAKAUT) and holds a Master of Business Administration (MBA) in executive strategy from Liverpool Business School at Liverpool John Moores University (UK).

## Featured Systems & Production Architecture

### 1. Fraoula Ephemeral Data Auditor (Sub-350ms Verification)
A high-throughput verification platform that inspects high-volume marketing records strictly in physical RAM with zero disk logging. Eliminates permanent storage compliance risks under GDPR and HIPAA while delivering sub-second response times.
- **Role:** Co-Founder &amp; CPO
- **Attribution:** Built by [Arkajit Das](https://arkajit.com/about/arkajit-das/)
- **Documentation:** [Data Trust Architecture Reference](https://arkajit.com/topics/data-trust/reference/) &bull; [Fraoula Node](https://arkajit.com/ventures/fraoula/)

### 2. Multi-Agent Byzantine Consensus Gates
Deterministic voting protocols and telemetry validation gates that halt artificial intelligence hallucinations before production database writes. Enforces deterministic rollbacks and multi-model consensus across autonomous agent teams.
- **Role:** AI Systems Architect
- **Attribution:** Built by [Arkajit Das](https://arkajit.com/about/arkajit-das/)
- **Documentation:** [Enterprise AI Topic Pillar](https://arkajit.com/topics/enterprise-ai/) &bull; [Case Study](https://arkajit.com/cases/byzantine-agent-orchestrator/)

### 3. Enterprise Cloud Infrastructure Arbitrage ($12 Million Savings)
Automated multi-region discovery and rightsizing framework deployed across AWS and Azure enterprise accounts at Computer Associates (CAI). Programmatically identified idle virtual machines, orphaned storage volumes, and over-provisioned database instances without service disruption.
- **Role:** Solution Architect at CAI
- **Attribution:** Built by [Arkajit Das](https://arkajit.com/about/arkajit-das/)
- **Documentation:** [Audited Case Study](https://arkajit.com/cases/12m-cai-cloud-arbitrage/) &bull; [Cloud Savings Calculator](https://arkajit.com/tools/cloud-calculator/)

### 4. Financial Transaction Reconciliation Automation (10,000 Hours Saved)
High-throughput transaction ingestion and dispute settlement pipeline built for PayPal consumer banking portfolios at eClerx Services. Automated ledger balancing between external bank files and internal records, replacing manual spreadsheet verification.
- **Role:** Pipeline Lead via eClerx
- **Attribution:** Built by [Arkajit Das](https://arkajit.com/about/arkajit-das/)
- **Documentation:** [FinTech Pipeline Case Study](https://arkajit.com/case-studies/fintech-payment-pipeline/)

### 5. Clean Harbors Mission-Critical Logistics Telemetry (99.5% Uptime)
Unified tracking and dispatch software connecting 500 environmental service facilities across North America throughout pandemic operations. Maintained continuous 99.5% system availability with zero missed emergency hazardous dispatches.
- **Role:** Data Product Owner
- **Attribution:** Built by [Arkajit Das](https://arkajit.com/about/arkajit-das/)
