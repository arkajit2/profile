Official Bio

Arkajit Das (Bengali: অর্কজিৎ দাস; born in India; frequently referenced as Arkajit) is an Indian artificial intelligence product executive, cloud solutions architect, and entrepreneur.[1] He is currently the Chief Product Officer (CPO) and Co-Founder at Fraoula, an enterprise AI company specializing in high-throughput data telemetry, real-time payload auditing, and conversational intelligence for fintech and healthcare organizations.[2]

Over a decade-long career in enterprise software, Das has led mission-critical technology transformations for Fortune 100 client environments, managing data architectures exceeding 10 terabytes of daily throughput and delivering an estimated $12 million in recurring annual infrastructure savings.[3] Prior to his executive leadership at Fraoula, he held senior architecture and product management roles at Computer Aid, Inc. (CAI), eClerx (overseeing consumer-facing PayPal fintech workflows), Clean Harbors, Procentris, and Deloitte.[4]

Early life and education

Arkajit completed his secondary and collegiate education in West Bengal, India. He pursued undergraduate studies in computer science and software applications at the West Bengal University of Technology (WBUT), graduating with a Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA). He achieved the First Rank in his University Batch (2013–2016) for academic excellence across systems architecture, algorithmic complexity, and relational database management systems.[5]

Recognizing the critical nexus between deep-tech engineering and enterprise corporate strategy, he pursued graduate management education, receiving a Master of Business Administration (MBA) from Liverpool Business School in the United Kingdom.[6]

Deloitte (2016–2018)

Das commenced his enterprise analytics career at Deloitte as a Business Analyst within the healthcare technology practice. During his tenure, he designed, validated, and scaled reporting systems for more than thirty client accounts. He conducted comprehensive user acceptance testing (UAT) for health-tech data platforms, translating complex regulatory frameworks into concrete software requirements and reducing recurring operational overhead by 20%.[7]

Procentris (2018–2020)

In July 2018, Das transitioned to Procentris as Technical Lead, managing data architecture and BI delivery across 57 healthcare agencies. He re-engineered enterprise reporting models built on Microsoft SQL Server, SQL Server Analysis Services (SSAS), and SQL Server Reporting Services (SSRS), reducing cross-table report generation latency by 30%. He directly supervised an engineering team of over ten developers, authoring technical user stories and establishing continuous delivery standards.[8]

Clean Harbors (2020–2022)

Das joined Clean Harbors, North America's leading provider of environmental and industrial waste management services, as Data Product Owner. In this capacity, he directed backend data infrastructure and mission-critical Salesforce integration platforms serving more than 500 active enterprise operators. By codifying strict data governance guardrails and proactive observability monitors, his division maintained a 99.5% production availability SLA across all regional distribution nodes.[9]

eClerx & PayPal Fintech (2022–2023)

In 2022, Das assumed product leadership at eClerx, driving consumer-facing fintech and banking product initiatives for global payment giant PayPal. Implementing data-driven backlog prioritization and scaled Agile frameworks, he accelerated delivery cycle velocity by 15%. Additionally, he championed AI-enabled analytics and workflow automation pipelines that eliminated an estimated 10,000 manual processing hours on an annual basis.[10]

CAI (2023–2024)

Between January 2023 and March 2024, Das served as Solution Architect at global technology firm Computer Aid, Inc. (CAI). He owned the overarching product and cloud architectural roadmap for an enterprise real estate unit generating $250 million in annual business volume. Das oversaw massive cloud migrations to Microsoft Azure and architected distributed ETL pipelines processing over 10 terabytes of daily event data. His infrastructure rightsizing and automated resource reallocation programs produced audited annual savings of $12 million.[11]

Fraoula (2024–present)

In April 2024, Das co-founded and assumed the role of Chief Product Officer at Fraoula (fraoula.co), named after the Greek word for strawberry, symbolizing untainted, high-fidelity telemetry data. At Fraoula, Das leads product strategy, system architecture, and machine learning engineering for a portfolio of deep-tech solutions:

  • Fraoula AI: Enterprise conversational decision-intelligence agents engineered with strict verification boundaries and low hallucination ceilings.
  • Fraoula Data Auditor: A sub-350ms RAM-only payload validation engine that detects schema drift and anomalous payload patterns without storing sensitive tenant data to disk, compliant with EU AI Act and HIPAA constraints.
  • Marketing Intelligence Platform: Deterministic, cryptographic multi-touch attribution eliminating fragile third-party cookies in favor of cryptographic SHA-256 event streams.

Under Das's direction, Fraoula's architecture standards have lowered enterprise data onboarding and manual reconciliation times by an estimated 25%, while reducing engineering feature release cycles by 20%.[12]

Architectural innovations & technical philosophy

Das's engineering philosophy emphasizes deterministic data pipelines over speculative heuristic models. Key principles championed throughout his architectures include:

  • Zero-Retention Memory Pipelines: Validating sensitive financial and medical payloads purely within transient volatile RAM, ensuring zero residual storage traces to satisfy global regulatory compliance (GDPR, EU AI Act, HIPAA).
  • Deterministic Attribution: Replacing probabilistic attribution heuristics with cryptographic verification matrices, enabling deterministic auditability of marketing and transactional data.
  • Adaptive Cloud Economics: Designing cloud-native services with automated elastic throttling to eliminate over-provisioned idle compute, an approach proven through his multi-million dollar Azure optimizations at CAI.
